Platform — No Student Left Behind

My platform focuses on building peer-to-peer support systems, cross-faculty collaboration, and open study spaces so everyone can access the help they need.

Peer-to-Peer Network

Organize mentorship groups and study buddies so students can learn from each other — not compete in silence.

Open Study Sessions

Weekly help corners (on-campus + online) for assignments, exams, and module reviews.

Cross-Faculty Collaboration

Connect skills across departments — tech helping design, design helping marketing, and so on.

Student Empowerment

Provide workshops and short tutorials focused on practical skills and study strategies.

Mental & Academic Support

Encourage asking for help, normalize failure as part of learning, and provide resources to bounce back.
